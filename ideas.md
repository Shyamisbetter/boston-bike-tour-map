## Idea 1: Cartographic Modernism
**Design Movement**: Swiss/International Cartographic Design meets Cycling Culture
**Core Principles**: Clean data hierarchy, map-first layout, functional minimalism, high contrast
**Color Philosophy**: Dark charcoal base (#1a1a2e) with neon accent colors for each certification type — electric green for LEED, warm amber for GBS, cyan for WELL, magenta for LBC, violet for Passive House. The dark base lets the map and data glow.
**Layout Paradigm**: Full-bleed map occupying 100% viewport with a collapsible glass-morphism sidebar. No traditional page structure — the map IS the page.
**Signature Elements**: (1) Frosted glass panels that float over the map, (2) Animated route traces that draw themselves when selected
**Interaction Philosophy**: Direct manipulation — click, drag, hover on the map itself. Minimal chrome, maximum data.
**Animation**: Route lines animate with a dash-offset drawing effect. Panels slide in with spring physics. Building markers pulse subtly on hover.
**Typography System**: Space Grotesk for headings (geometric, modern), Inter for body/data (neutral, readable at small sizes)

## Idea 2: Urban Field Guide
**Design Movement**: Editorial/Magazine Design meets Field Guide Illustration
**Core Principles**: Storytelling through data, warm approachability, layered information, discovery-driven
**Color Philosophy**: Warm cream background (#faf8f5) with earthy greens (#2d5016, #4a7c2e), terracotta (#c4622d), and slate blue (#3d5a80). Feels like a printed cycling guide you'd tuck in your jersey pocket.
**Layout Paradigm**: Split view — left panel is a scrollable "field guide" with route cards, building profiles, and stats; right side is the interactive map. The guide panel feels like a book.
**Signature Elements**: (1) Hand-drawn style dividers and subtle paper texture, (2) Building "profile cards" with certification badges styled like stamps
**Interaction Philosophy**: Browse and discover — scroll the guide, and the map follows. Click a building on the map, and the guide scrolls to its profile.
**Animation**: Smooth scroll-linked map panning. Cards expand with a gentle accordion. Route highlights fade in with a warm glow.
**Typography System**: Fraunces for display headings (organic, warm serif), Source Sans 3 for body (clean, readable)

## Idea 3: Data Dashboard Noir
**Design Movement**: Bloomberg Terminal meets Cycling Analytics
**Core Principles**: Information density, real-time feel, professional authority, data-forward
**Color Philosophy**: Near-black background (#0d1117) with a matrix of accent colors — bright green (#39d353) for active routes, blue (#58a6ff) for infrastructure, warm yellow (#e3b341) for highlights. Feels like a mission control for bike tour planning.
**Layout Paradigm**: Multi-panel dashboard with resizable panes — map center stage, stats bar across top, route selector on left, building detail on right. Every pixel carries data.
**Signature Elements**: (1) Glowing data counters that tick up as layers load, (2) Mini-map overview in corner showing full route context
**Interaction Philosophy**: Power-user controls — keyboard shortcuts, layer toggles, filter dropdowns. Dense but navigable.
**Animation**: Counter animations on stats, smooth layer transitions with opacity fades, route traces with animated dashes.
**Typography System**: JetBrains Mono for data/stats (monospace precision), Outfit for headings and labels (geometric, modern)
